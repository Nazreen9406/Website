FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions

These FAQs are designed to provide a better understanding of our GVMC online services. They often link to more detailed information. If you have any questions about our services that are not covered here or elsewhere on our website, please email ourgvmc@yahoo.co.in.

How to pay Trade License?

  1. Enter website address: click here
  2. Select Payments menu and Select Pay trade license.
  3. You will be transferred to the trade enquiry page. Enter your valid Shop Id to get total demand and collection for the given shop id number.
  4. After getting the details, a "Click here to pay trade license" button will appear on the screen.
  5. After clicking that button, you will be transferred to the payment mode of easy2pay, where you enter debit/credit card details, email, mobile number, and word verification, then submit.
  6. After successful payment, you will be transferred to another page with the certificate. Print it and keep the license for future reference.

How to pay Property Tax & Water Charges Through Online?

  1. Enter website address: click here
  2. Select Payments menu and Select Pay Property Tax.
  3. You will be transferred to the tax enquiry page. Enter your house number to get total demand and collection for the given assessment number.
  4. After getting the details, a "Click here to pay property tax" button will appear on the screen.
  5. After clicking, you will be transferred to the payment mode of easy2pay, where you enter debit/credit card details, email, mobile number, and word verification, then submit.
  6. After successful payment, you will be transferred to another page with the payment receipt. Print and save the receipt for your records.
